# ApexShock: Hero Roster & Combat Archetypes

Meet the six original Vanguard Champions competing in the Solaris Rift arena.

---

## 1. Volt (Kinetic Shock Tank)
- **Role**: Frontline Disruptor / Kinetic Tank
- **Base Vitality**: 4,200 HP
- **Weapon**: Twin Arc-Reactor Shockwave Gauntlets
- **Super Ability**: *Thunderbolt Meteor Leap* - Leaps into the targeted area, knocking back enemies and dealing 650 AoE shockwave damage.
- **Star Passive**: *Overclocked Capacitors* - Taking damage builds up static energy, discharging a periodic electrical shock to nearby foes.

---

## 2. Valkyrie (Solar Marksman)
- **Role**: Long-Range Sniper / Precision Marksman
- **Base Vitality**: 3,600 HP
- **Weapon**: Twin-Rail Sun-Lance Beam Rifle
- **Super Ability**: *Supernova Piercing Volley* - Fires an ultra-dense solar lance that penetrates walls and hits all enemies in a straight line.
- **Star Passive**: *Aegis Resonance* - Standing still for 1.5 seconds increases attack range by 20% and adds armor-piercing damage.

---

## 3. Cinder (Magma Artillery)
- **Role**: Area Denial / Siege Artillery
- **Base Vitality**: 3,000 HP
- **Weapon**: Pressurized Brass Magma Core Mortar
- **Super Ability**: *Volcanic Eruption* - Lobs an explosive cluster bomb creating lingering lava pools that burn targets over time.
- **Star Passive**: *Pressurized Warheads* - Direct projectile hits apply a burning status effect reducing enemy healing by 40%.

---

## 4. Bastion (Siege Juggernaut)
- **Role**: Heavy Anchor / Close-Quarters Brawler
- **Base Vitality**: 5,200 HP
- **Weapon**: Tri-Barrel Sonic Scatter Cannon
- **Super Ability**: *Aegis Barrier Dome* - Deploys a stationary energy bubble shield absorbing up to 2,500 incoming damage.
- **Star Passive**: *Titanium Bulkhead* - Reduces all incoming explosive and projectile damage by 15% when below 50% HP.

---

## 5. Tesla (Arc Technomancer)
- **Role**: Support & Crowd Control / Cyber Alchemist
- **Base Vitality**: 3,400 HP
- **Weapon**: Chain-Lightning Conduit Staff
- **Super Ability**: *Overcharge Pylon* - Summons an automated shock turret targeting the lowest-health enemy within range.
- **Star Passive**: *Harmonic Frequency* - Chain lightning jumps heal allied team members for 30% of the damage dealt.

---

## 6. Phantom (Void Skirmisher)
- **Role**: Flanker / Stealth Assassin
- **Base Vitality**: 3,400 HP
- **Weapon**: Dual Corrosive Phase Daggers
- **Super Ability**: *Void Blink Ambush* - Teleports behind the targeted enemy, becoming invisible for 2.0 seconds with +50% critical strike chance.
- **Star Passive**: *Phantom Stride* - Moving through stealth flora bushes grants +35% movement speed and temporary phase shielding.
